Evaluation Framework

What happens after you submit?

Our transparent, milestone-driven approach to reviewing client proposals.

🔒 Mutual Confidentiality

All RFP documentation, architecture sketches, and project concepts submitted are treated as strictly confidential under default non-disclosure protocol.

📐 Technical Feasibility

Our senior systems architects review your scope to recommend clean, reusable building blocks and flag potential bottlenecks early.

📋 Detailed Proposal

You receive an itemized proposal containing a phased delivery roadmap, milestone deliverables, technology stack options, and transparent pricing.

⏱️ 3-Day Turnaround

For standard institutional RFPs, our initial review and scoping questions are returned to your team within 3 business days.
