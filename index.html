Independent non-profit, supported by VOTESS

Nayibeej works for everyone, in every category.

Nayibeej supports people and communities across all social categories. It has its own board, funds and mission. VOTESS supports it and does not own it.

What VOTESS provides

  • Technology, website and app help
  • Administrative support
  • Volunteer time and sponsorship
  • All of it documented and valued fairly

What stays with Nayibeej

  • Choice of programmes and priorities
  • Its own bank account and books
  • Donor receipts and reports
  • Every surplus, applied to its mission
