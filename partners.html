Partnership models

Flexible engagement frameworks designed for software distributors, system integrators, and social enterprises.

Reseller / Channel

Expand your portfolio by offering VOTESS brands and technology to your client base. Ideal for education consultants, IT service agencies, and regional software distributors looking to offer Nexus ERP and enterprise tools.

  • Attractive recurring revenue share and margin tiers.
  • Full sales enablement, co-branded collateral & demo sandbox.
  • Dedicated tier-2 partner support and pre-sales assistance.

Technology Integration

Integrate our enterprise systems and APIs seamlessly into your existing platforms. Connect banking gateways, biometric attendance hardware, travel distribution GDS feeds, or custom CRM solutions.

  • RESTful APIs, real-time webhooks & SDK libraries.
  • Developer sandboxes with automated test suites.
  • Direct engineering escalation channel for architecture queries.

White-label Solutions

Leverage our robust backend systems under your own brand identity. Deploy enterprise-ready school portals, booking engines, or customer messaging hubs fully branded with your corporate colors and domain.

  • Custom domain CNAME mapping with automated SSL certs.
  • Brand-customized mobile applications (iOS and Android).
  • Complete backend abstraction managed by VOTESS SRE teams.
