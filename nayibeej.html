About Nayibeej

For everyone. In every category.

Nayibeej believes that good work in one area of society lifts all areas. Its mission is to provide practical support — through technology, coordination, and resources — to individuals and communities across all social categories.

It is not a charity arm of VOTESS. It has its own board, its own bank account, its own donor relationships and its own set of priorities. VOTESS provides technology help, administrative support and volunteer time. Nayibeej decides how and where that goes.

The key distinction

✅ VOTESS supports Nayibeej

Technology, website, apps, admin support, volunteering, sponsorship — all documented and valued fairly.

❌ VOTESS does not own Nayibeej

Nayibeej chooses its programmes, controls its funds, issues its own donor receipts and applies every surplus to its mission.

Impact areas

Where Nayibeej works.

Nayibeej's board decides where and how to work. These are the broad social categories it aims to serve.

📚

Education

Learning portals, school software and resource-sharing platforms for schools in underserved areas. Every child deserves access to quality learning tools.

🏥

Healthcare

Medical camp records, health reminders, and awareness platforms. Simple digital tools can dramatically improve outcomes for community health programmes.

👩‍👧

Women & Child Welfare

Secure registration, case management and private messaging systems for programmes supporting the safety, nutrition and empowerment of women and children.

💼

Livelihoods & Skills

Enrolment systems, attendance tracking, progress reports and certification platforms for vocational training and skill development programmes.

🌾

Farmer Support

Advisory messaging, weather alerts, market price information and government scheme notifications delivered directly to farmers via SMS and web portals.

🌿

Environment

Volunteer coordination, tree plantation tracking, waste management awareness and conservation campaign dashboards for environmental initiatives.

🆘

Disaster Relief

Fast messaging systems, donation coordination pages, volunteer dispatch tools and beneficiary tracking for emergency response and rehabilitation.

🧓

Elder Care

Companion apps, welfare check systems and community programme portals for elderly individuals who need connection, information and support.

Transparency

VOTESS and Nayibeej: what goes where.

The relationship is deliberately structured so that business success and social purpose never blur together.

What VOTESS provides

  • Technology infrastructure — hosting, website, apps
  • Administrative and back-office support
  • Volunteer time from VOTESS employees
  • Financial sponsorship (documented at fair value)
  • Access to VOTESS's enterprise software platforms
  • Introductions to partner organisations

What stays with Nayibeej

  • Choice of programmes, priorities and partners
  • Its own bank account and financial records
  • All donor receipts and donor relationships
  • Governance — its own independent board
  • Every rupee of surplus, applied to its mission
  • Public accountability and annual reporting
