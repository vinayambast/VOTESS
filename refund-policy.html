Fair Business Standard: VOTESS is committed to building transparent, reliable relationships. This policy defines the terms under which refunds, subscription terminations, and project adjustments are evaluated and processed.

1. General Framework & Scope

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all commercial engagements, software licensing, SaaS access fees, and digital services procured through votess.in, votess.com, or via signed enterprise Statements of Work (SOW).

2. SaaS & Cloud Subscription Products

For recurring subscription models across our branded software platforms:

A. Nexus ERP (Educational Platform)

  • Monthly / Annual Subscriptions: Subscriptions may be cancelled by institutional administrators at any time prior to the renewal date via the administrative console or by written notice to info@votess.in.
  • Cancellation Effect: Cancellations take effect at the conclusion of the active billing period. Access to portal modules and cloud hosting remains fully functional until the end of the paid cycle.
  • Refund Eligibility: Because cloud infrastructure, server capacity, and database backups are provisioned in advance, recurring subscription fees are non-refundable for the elapsed portion of any billing cycle once commenced.
  • Annual Contract Termination: In the event of early termination of multi-year contracts, refunds are calculated pro-rata after reverting any multi-year discount incentives, subject to a 30-day notice period.

B. Ciao Chat (Communication & Engagement)

  • Base monthly platform tiers may be terminated at the close of any calendar month.
  • Prepaid SMS gateways, WhatsApp Business API credits, and telecommunication carrier usage fees are consumable third-party expenses and are strictly non-refundable once loaded into active carrier pools.

C. Non-Profit ERP (Early Access / Beta Programs)

  • Pre-registration fees or early-access beta onboarding deposits are 100% refundable up until the official platform deployment date upon written request.

3. GoVacayTrip (Travel Vertical)

GoVacayTrip serves as a technology platform connecting travelers with certified airlines, hotels, holiday operators, and bus carriers:

  • Flight & Hotel Bookings: All ticket cancellations, date changes, and refunds are subject to the fare rules and policies of the respective airline, hotel chain, or service provider.
  • Convenience & Platform Processing Fees: Third-party payment gateway charges and non-refundable platform convenience fees are deducted from gross refund payouts in accordance with DGCA and carrier rules.
  • Processing Period: Travel refunds approved by carriers are credited back to the traveler within 7 to 10 business days following carrier remittance.

4. Custom Enterprise Development & Consulting Services

For custom digital transformation, web application engineering, and enterprise architecture contracts:

  • Milestone-Based Settlement: Custom projects are divided into structured milestones (e.g., Discovery, Architecture, Phase 1 MVP, UAT, Deployment). Payments made for successfully accepted milestones are deemed fully earned and non-refundable.
  • Project Cancellation: If a client requests contract termination mid-milestone, billing is calculated on a verified Time & Materials (T&M) basis for engineering hours expended up to the official termination notice date. Any unearned retainer or prepaid balance will be promptly refunded.

5. Strictly Non-Refundable Items

The following categories are strictly excluded from refund eligibility:

  • Domain name registration, renewal, or transfer fees incurred with domain registrars.
  • Custom third-party SSL certificates, dedicated IP allocations, or server provisioning fees paid to third-party data centers.
  • Completed training, architectural consulting, or workshop hours already delivered.
  • Setup or data migration onboarding fees once migration engineering has commenced.

6. Step-by-Step Cancellation & Refund Request Process

To ensure swift and orderly handling of any cancellation or refund request, please follow these steps:

Step Action Timeline
1. Written Notice Email our Billing Team at info@votess.in with your Account ID, Invoice Number, and detailed reason for request. Immediate
2. Review & Audit Our finance and technical support teams audit system logs, deliverable status, and transaction validation. 2 - 3 Business Days
3. Decision Notification You receive written confirmation detailing approved refund amounts, deductions (if any), or clarification questions. Within 4 Business Days
4. Payout Processing Approved funds are initiated via the original payment source (Card, Net Banking, UPI, or Bank Wire). 5 - 7 Business Days

7. Payout Methods & Banking Timelines

All approved refunds are credited back strictly to the original payment method used during the initial transaction (e.g., credit card, corporate net banking, UPI, or NEFT/RTGS). For security and anti-money laundering compliance, cash refunds or transfers to alternate third-party accounts are strictly prohibited.

8. Chargebacks & Dispute Escalation

We encourage all clients to communicate directly with our Billing Support before initiating a bank chargeback or payment gateway dispute. Unilateral chargebacks initiated on active services without prior notice may trigger automated suspension of associated cloud accounts pending dispute resolution.
