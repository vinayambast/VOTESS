Overview: This Cookie Policy outlines how VOTESS ("we", "us") deploys cookies, local storage, and similar web technologies across our web properties to guarantee site security, maintain user sessions, and analyze anonymized performance metrics.

1. What Are Cookies & Tracking Technologies?

Cookies are small alphanumeric text files stored on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you navigate modern websites. Cookies allow websites to remember your device, preserve session state (such as light/dark mode preference or active login tokens), and ensure responsive navigation.

In addition to cookies, we may use related browser mechanisms such as HTML5 Local Storage, Session Storage, and server log identifiers to maintain reliable enterprise performance.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

We classify all cookies into four distinct operational categories:

Category Purpose & Functionality Necessity
Strictly Necessary Cookies Essential for fundamental website operations, CSRF protection, secure authentication, and CDN routing. Without these, the site cannot function. Mandatory (Cannot be disabled)
Functional & Preference Cookies Preserve your customized settings, such as your chosen light/dark visual theme, chosen region, or temporary form field drafts. Recommended
Performance & Analytics Cookies Collect aggregate, non-identifying telemetry on visitor counts, average page load times, bounce rates, and navigation errors to improve user experience. Optional (Can opt-out)
Security & Integrity Cookies Detect automated bot attacks, rate-limit excessive API calls, and maintain encrypted session integrity across payment steps. Mandatory for Security

4. Third-Party Cookies & Integrations

Some cookies are deployed by vetted third-party enterprise providers when you interact with specific features:

  • Cloudflare & Content Delivery Networks: Distribute assets globally, cache static content, and protect against distributed cyber threats.
  • Payment Gateways (e.g., Razorpay/Stripe): Utilize secure tokens to safeguard credit card fraud detection during online payments.
  • Web Analytics Services: Help our engineering team diagnose broken links, layout rendering times, and device compatibility.

5. How You Can Control & Disable Cookies

You have full authority over cookie storage on your device. You can manage or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Settings > Safari > Advanced > Privacy.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions.

Note: If you choose to reject or block strictly necessary cookies or local storage, certain features of our site (such as theme persistence, secure contact submissions, or client portals) may not function as intended.

6. Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Our website respects automated browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) headers. When detected, optional analytics and tracking scripts are automatically suppressed.
