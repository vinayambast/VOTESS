1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Virtual Operating Technology & Enterprise Systems ("VOTESS", "we", "us", or "our") collects, stores, processes, discloses, and protects information gathered through our websites (including votess.in, votess.com), digital products (Nexus ERP, GoVacayTrip, Ciao Chat, Non-Profit ERP), and enterprise software services.

By using our services, you acknowledge the collection and processing of your information in accordance with this Policy, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), the Information Technology Act 2000, and where applicable, global data protection standards such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver efficient enterprise services, communicate with partners, and maintain system integrity:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Identity & Contact Information: Full name, business email address, phone number, postal address, company/organisation name, job title, and country.
  • Inquiries & RFP Submissions: Details of project scopes, budgetary ranges, RFP documents, operational requirements, and correspondence submitted via our forms or chatbot.
  • Account & Authentication Credentials: Usernames, encrypted passwords, two-factor authentication tokens, and profile preferences on our platform portals.
  • Billing & Transaction Details: Tax IDs (GST/PAN), billing address, invoicing data, and payment verification records (credit/debit card numbers are handled via RBI-authorized payment processors and never stored directly on our servers).

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical & Network Data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, time-zone settings, and network provider.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Pages visited, clickstream patterns, navigation flow, duration on pages, error logs, referring URLs, and feature interactions.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: Session identifiers and preference states as detailed in our Cookie Policy.

4. How We Share & Disclose Information

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. We only share data under the following strictly controlled scenarios:

  • Authorised Infrastructure Providers: Cloud hosting (AWS, Google Cloud), managed database providers, email gateway providers (SMTP/Transactional), and customer relationship platforms operating under strict data processing agreements.
  • Payment Processors & Banking Partners: PCI-DSS certified payment gateways for secure billing, GST compliance, and transaction verification.
  • Nayibeej Non-Profit Coordination: If you voluntarily submit a request to volunteer or partner with the independent non-profit Nayibeej, we transfer your inquiry directly to Nayibeej's independent team with your consent.
  • Legal & Regulatory Requirements: When strictly required by law, subpoena, regulatory authority, or court order under Indian or international jurisdiction.

5. Data Security & Retention

We employ enterprise-grade administrative, technical, and physical safeguards:

  • End-to-end SSL/TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit across our websites and APIs.
  • AES-256 encryption at rest for sensitive data, database backups, and credentials.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensuring only vetted personnel have access to specific datasets.
  • Regular automated security auditing, vulnerability assessments, and DDoS protection layers.

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal, tax, or statutory retention requirements (typically up to 7 years for financial records), and resolve disputes.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (under DPDP Act, GDPR, or relevant regulations), you enjoy the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Summary: Request a clear summary of personal data held and processed by VOTESS.
  • Right to Correction & Updation: Request correction of inaccurate, misleading, or outdated personal information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your data when no longer needed for legal or contractual purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent previously granted for specific non-contractual processing activities.
  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your structured personal data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email our Privacy Team at info@votess.in. We respond to all verified requests within 30 days.
